# "Building From Scratch" — Faceless Launch Content Plan

**Persona:** A corporate executive, father of four, who has never posted on social media before and knows nothing about AI. Starting from zero — no idea, no audience, no experience — to see if AI can help him finally build a business while keeping his day job and staying present for his kids.

**Format:** Faceless account (no face on camera). Everything below is designed around text-on-screen, screen recordings, notes-app screenshots, and voiceover-free or voice-altered narration — not talking-head video.

**Voice:** Honest, unpolished, first-person, slightly self-deprecating. Never "guru" energy. The whole hook is that he's a beginner in public, at his own pace — no artificial deadline.

---

## Brand setup (do this before Post 1)

- **Handle:** something literal and searchable — e.g. `@buildingfromscratch`, `@corporatedadbuilds`, `@fourkidsonebusiness`, `@buildingwhilecorporate`. Pick one and stay consistent.
- **Bio:**
  > Corporate exec. Dad of 4. Never posted before.
  > Using AI to build a business — job + kids intact.
  > Entry 1 👇
- **Visual style in Canva:** pick ONE template system and reuse it for every post so the account looks intentional:
  - Dark navy or charcoal background, one accent color (e.g. warm orange or green), bold sans-serif (Canva: "Anton," "Poppins Bold," or "Archivo Black" for headlines, "Inter" for body).
  - Recurring frame: small tag in the corner every post — "Entry X" or a date — so the ongoing log itself becomes a hook, without committing to a fixed end date.
  - Use Canva's "Notes app" / "iMessage" / "sticky note" mockup elements for authenticity (screenshots of his own notes, calendar, ChatGPT chats).

## Notes for filling this in as you go

- Posts 9 and 10 have bracketed placeholders — don't invent a fake milestone. Wait for the real thing and drop it in; the account's whole credibility is that it's true.
- Keep the "Entry X" counter consistent on every single post (small corner tag in Canva) — it's the visual through-line that makes people follow to see how it unfolds, without boxing you into a fixed deadline.
- Post cadence suggestion: 2-3x/week is sustainable given the "5-6 hours/week" time budget established in Post 5 — don't commit to daily, it'll break the schedule you just publicly promised to respect.
- Once you pick the actual handle/name, swap it into the Post 1 caption and bio above.
